# Interior Props Engine

Procedural architectural interior furnishings, commercial office workstations, and storage systems.

## Components
- **DeskGen**: Contemporary executive desks, minimal study tables, and drawer pedestals with modesty panels and cable grommets.
- **ChairGen**: Ergonomic 5-star swivel mesh office chairs, dining chairs, and padded club armchairs with castors.
- **ShelfGen**: Modular open bookcases, industrial wire shelving, and tiered storage with procedural book, binder, and decor placement.

## Shaders & Profiles
- `desk.comp`: Desktop chamfer extrusion, tubular leg frame sweep, and drawer face recess synthesis.
- `chair.comp`: Ergonomic lumbar curve lofting, star-base radial arm sweep, and caster wheel placement.
- `shelf.comp`: Upright panel slicing, horizontal shelf board arrays, and object cluster distribution.
- `desk_profile.h`: Desktop thickness, bevel radius, and leg frame dimensions.
- `chair_profile.h`: Seat cushion profile, lumbar curve, and 5-star base radial coordinates.
- `shelf_profile.h`: Upright thickness, shelf spacing, and crown molding profile.
