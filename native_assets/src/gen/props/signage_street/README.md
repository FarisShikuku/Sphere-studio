# Signage Street Props Engine

Procedural roadside signage, highway directional information boards, and milestone markers.

## Components
- **InfoBoardGen**: Highway green, tourist brown, and interstate blue directional information boards with H-frame steel support posts and rain hoods.
- **KilometreMarkerGen**: Arched limestone highway milestones and modern flexible retroreflective delineator guide posts.

## Shaders & Profiles
- `info_board.comp`: Extruded aluminum sign frame, retroreflective sheeting, and structural beam synthesis.
- `km_marker.comp`: Milestone chamfering, dome cap lofting, and reflector lens placement.
- `info_board_profile.h`: Structural H-beam and Z-bar stiffener profile definitions.
- `km_marker_profile.h`: Classical Roman milestone arch profile.
