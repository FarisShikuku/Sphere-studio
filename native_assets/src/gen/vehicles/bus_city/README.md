# City Transit Buses (`bus_city/`)

Generates 4 municipal & rapid transit city buses:
1. `BUS_CITY_STANDARD` (12m 78-passenger municipal bus with dual sliding doors)
2. `BUS_CITY_DOUBLE_DECKER` (4.38m twin-deck 104-passenger transit bus with dual front windshields)
3. `BUS_CITY_ARTICULATED_BENDY` (18m 145-passenger articulated bus joined by pleated rubber bellows)
4. `BUS_CITY_LOW_FLOOR` (Electric BRT low-floor bus with roof battery shroud & zero-step boarding)
