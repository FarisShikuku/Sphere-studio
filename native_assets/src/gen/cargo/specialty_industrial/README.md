# Specialty Industrial Procedural Commodity Module

Generates heavy industrial freight: rapid-hardening cement bags, 6-meter structural steel pipes, palletized fired bricks, and heavy commercial tyre stacks.

## Supported Variants
- `CEMENT_BAG_50KG`: High-density rapid-hardening construction Portland cement 50kg.
- `STEEL_PIPE_6M`: 6.0m schedule 40 structural seamless carbon steel line pipe with protective yellow caps.
- `BRICK_PALLET`: Palletized stack of 400 facing clay bricks bound with tensioned polyester strapping.
- `TYRE_STACK`: Vertical column of 4 all-position heavy truck commercial radial tyres.
