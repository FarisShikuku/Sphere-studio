# Raw Materials Procedural Commodity Module

Generates industrial and infrastructure raw supplies: forestry hardwood logs, milled dimensional timber, structural steel rebar, and bulk aggregate mounds.

## Supported Variants
- `WOOD_LOG_OAK`: 2.4m natural oak tree trunk with bark furrow displacement and sawn end rings.
- `TIMBER_PLANK`: 3.0m pack of planed, kiln-dried structural construction lumber.
- `STEEL_REBAR`: Banded bundle of high-yield ribbed carbon steel reinforcing bars.
- `COAL_PILE`: High-carbon anthracite lump coal volumetric mound.
- `GRAVEL_PILE`: Crushed granite coarse aggregate road-base pile.
