# Boxes & Crates Procedural Commodity Module

Generates corrugated shipping cartons, slatted wooden crates, plastic harvest bins, and standardized Euro pallets for warehouses, shipping lines, and retail distribution.

## Supported Variants
- `CARDBOARD_BOX`: Standard corrugated brown shipping carton with tape and barcode.
- `WOODEN_CRATE`: Heavy slatted pine machinery crate with corner reinforcement.
- `PLASTIC_CRATE`: Molded HDPE agricultural vented produce crate.
- `PALLET`: Standard EUR-1 four-way wooden logistics exchange pallet (1200x800mm).
- `MILK_CRATE`: Rigid polypropylene stackable milk crate.
