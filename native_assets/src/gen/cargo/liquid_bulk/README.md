# Liquid & Bulk Carriers Procedural Commodity Module

Generates industrial drums, road tankers, chemical jerricans, milk churns, and pressurized cylinders for process industries, logistics, and agriculture.

## Supported Variants
- `WATER_TANKER`: 8,000L potable water road transport tanker module.
- `OIL_DRUM_200L`: 200L (55 gallon) tight-head steel chemical drum with rolling hoop ribs.
- `JERRICAN_20L`: 20L high-density polyethylene fuel/water carrier with handle.
- `MILK_CHURN`: 40L sanitary stainless steel dairy churn with lid.
- `GAS_CYLINDER`: 15kg liquefied petroleum gas (LPG) pressurized steel bottle.
